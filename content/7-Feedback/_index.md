> Here, I would like to share my personal perspectives on the wonderful experience of participating in the First Cloud Journey (FCAJ) program.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is highly open and dynamic. The FCAJ community and seniors are always enthusiastic in helping whenever I encounter issues with cloud services or system errors, even outside of regular working hours. The online collaboration spaces and shared documentation are extremely clear, helping me focus entirely on my project.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors provide highly detailed guidance, especially concerning complex architectural components like configuring security or orchestrating microservices. The admin team ensures timely support for procedures and provides adequate resources. I particularly appreciate that the mentor always encourages me to research and read technical docs to troubleshoot independently rather than just giving away the solutions.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The architectural deployment tasks align perfectly with my Software Engineering major. Combining foundational university knowledge with practical technologies like AI/NLP models and Cloud Computing has solidified my architectural mindset and tremendously enhanced my practical engineering skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Throughout the internship, I learned how to design Serverless architectures, operate real-world systems, and manage a project lifecycle professionally. Additionally, teamwork, business analysis, and professional communication skills within a corporate setting significantly improved through each phase.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The spirit of collaboration and knowledge sharing within FCAJ is incredibly strong. Everyone works professionally, respects individual ideas, and is always ready to support each other during tough project deployment phases, making me feel like a truly integrated part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides flexible scheduling, allowing me to easily balance work with completing my university coursework. Being able to participate in knowledge-sharing meetups and gain access to a real-world system environment is a massive privilege.
